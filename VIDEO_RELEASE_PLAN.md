# ChromaOS v2.1.0 — Official Video Release Presentation Plan (Storyboard & Narration)

**Total Duration**: 75 Seconds (1:15)  
**Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 (Landscape) with 9:16 (Shorts/Reels) framing safety  
**Video Tone**: Elegant, refined, commercial, calm, and professional. Deep obsidian/graphite visual aesthetic with crisp typography and subtle micro-interactions.

## 🎬 Scene-by-Scene Storyboard & Script

### Scene 1: Introduction to ChromaOS
- **Timecode**: `00:00 - 00:12` (12 seconds)
- **Visual Description**: 
  - Starts with an ultra-close-up macro shot of a sleek Android smartphone resting on a dark walnut desk. The screen awakens with a gentle fade-in of the ChromaOS obsidian lock screen displaying the elegant split clock.
  - Camera pulls back smoothly to reveal the minimal phone silhouette in a dark studio setting.
- **On-Screen Captions**: 
  - `ChromaOS v2.1.0`
  - `A Human-Centred Android Launcher Experience`
- **Voiceover Narration**:
  > *"When technology is designed with care, it doesn't fight for your attention—it complements your rhythm. Introducing ChromaOS Version 2.1.0. A refined, human-centred launcher crafted for clarity, tactile satisfaction, and real performance on Android 10 through 16."*

### Scene 2: The Clean, Updated Home Screen Canvas
- **Timecode**: `00:12 - 00:25` (13 seconds)
- **Visual Description**:
  - The lock screen unlocks with a seamless upward gesture, revealing the pristine new home screen.
  - No visual clutter, no neon badges, and zero intrusive cards. 
  - A subtle slow-pan across the wallpaper showcases the deep charcoal texture and refined typography.
  - A hand gently presses and holds on the screen (`420ms`); a fluid tactile vibration ripple triggers the clean Widget Catalog drawer sliding up.
- **On-Screen Captions**:
  - `Pristine Clean Canvas`
  - `No Clutter • Tactile Long-Press Controls`
- **Voiceover Narration**:
  > *"We listened closely to your feedback and stripped away the noise. The legacy clutter and redundant buttons are gone. In their place is a pristine, intentional canvas that puts you in complete control. Add or remove widgets with an intuitive long-press."*

### Scene 3: The Real-Time Battery Indicator Widget
- **Timecode**: `00:25 - 00:40` (15 seconds)
- **Visual Description**:
  - Camera zooms in to focus on the Real-Time Battery widget.
  - An authentic USB-C cable is clicked into the phone's charging port in the frame.
  - Instantly, without delay, the battery icon animates into an active lightning bolt, the status updates dynamically to *"Charging (Fast AC)"*, and the battery percentage bar transitions smoothly with glowing precision.
  - A quick tap opens Android's native Battery Usage settings.
- **On-Screen Captions**:
  - `Real-Time Native Battery Telemetry`
  - `Instant Broadcast Sync • Charging & Health Diagnostics`
  - `Zero Simulated Numbers`
- **Voiceover Narration**:
  > *"Every single metric here is genuine. Powered by direct Android system broadcasts, the new Real-Time Battery indicator reflects your phone's exact level, charging state, temperature, and battery health in real-time. No approximations. No simulation."*

### Scene 4: Dynamic Bluetooth Device Indicator in Action
- **Timecode**: `00:40 - 00:54` (14 seconds)
- **Visual Description**:
  - The home screen is shown with no Bluetooth widget visible (completely hidden, clean).
  - A pair of true wireless earbuds are opened from their case in front of the device.
  - As soon as the Bluetooth connection is established, the Bluetooth Device Indicator widget glides gracefully into view on the canvas, showing the exact device name (`Sony WH-1000XM4`) and linked status.
  - The earbuds are placed back into the case and closed; the widget fades away instantly, returning the screen to a clean state.
- **On-Screen Captions**:
  - `Dynamic Bluetooth Indicator`
  - `Appears When Connected • Auto-Hides When Idle`
  - `Native Android 10–16 Compatibility`
- **Voiceover Narration**:
  > *"Connectivity should be smart, not persistent. Our Bluetooth device indicator appears only when a supported accessory is actively linked. Once you disconnect, the widget disappears automatically—keeping your home screen quiet and distraction-free."*
